What the app does

The app turns things you really do — movement, sleep, study, creating, practising — into a character with six stats. It reads evidence you choose to give it (photos and videos you submit, health data on your phone, accounts you connect or log in with) and an automated reviewer decides what that evidence is worth. Stats can go up when you show up and down when you stop. That is the game.
