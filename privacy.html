Privacy Policy

Virtual Me: Origins · Last updated 6 August 2026

Virtual Me: Origins turns things you really do into character stats. That only works if we can check evidence you send us — so this policy explains exactly what we collect, who processes it, and how to get rid of it.

Who we are

Virtual Me: Origins is developed by Richard de Souza. For any privacy question or request, contact richard@moneymapgroup.com.

Who can use the app

Virtual Me: Origins is for adults aged 18 and over. The app asks for your year of birth and will not let you continue if it indicates you are under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has used the app, email us and we will delete the account.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Photos and videos you submit as proof To verify that you did the action you committed to. Reviewed automatically by AI.
Body scan photos (front, sides, back, face) To generate your 3D avatar. Sent to our 3D generation providers.
Details you tell us — sex, year of birth, height Your stats are scored against people of a similar age and sex. Height is used to size your avatar correctly in connected games.
Your actions, habits, streaks and stats The core of the app — your character.
Health and fitness data (iPhone only, with your permission) Steps, workouts, flights climbed and heart data, read from Apple Health to verify physical actions. Read only — we never write to Apple Health.
Usernames you paste for services like Duolingo or GitHub To check public activity as evidence. We never ask for, or receive, your passwords for those services.
An anonymous account identifier, and your Apple ID if you choose to link it To keep your character attached to you. Linking is optional.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, address, phone number or payment card details.
  • We do not build face-recognition templates or any biometric identifier from your scans.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data. Ever.
  • We do not show advertising.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes below.

ProviderWhat they receive
Google Firebase (Google Cloud)Hosts the database, file storage and sign-in. Your account data and proof media are stored here.
AnthropicReviews your submitted proof and classifies the actions you write. Receives the proof media and the text of your action.
fal.ai and MeshyTurn your body scan photos into a 3D avatar. Receive the scan photos.

Connecting a game

When you scan a game's QR code, you choose to share a read-only snapshot of your character — your stats, active perks, connected app names, avatar files, and your height if you provided it. Nothing is shared until you scan a code, the code expires after five minutes, and a game can never write back to your account or sign in as you.

How long we keep it

Your character data and avatars are kept while your account exists, because the whole point is a record that lasts. Proof media is kept so a reward can always be traced back to its evidence. If you ask us to delete your account, all of it goes.

Deleting your data

Email richard@moneymapgroup.com from the address linked to your account, or include your account ID from the app's Settings screen, and we will delete your account, avatars, proof media and stats within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to how it is processed. Email us and we will help. You can also withdraw a permission at any time in your phone's settings — for example, revoking camera or health access.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with access rules that restrict each account's data to that account. No system is perfect, and we will tell affected users promptly if a breach ever occurs.

Testing releases

This app is currently distributed to a small number of invited testers through Apple TestFlight and Google Play internal testing. Test builds may change often and may contain features that are incomplete.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who processes it, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
