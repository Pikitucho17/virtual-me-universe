Privacy Policy

Virtual Me: Origins · Last updated 9 November 2026

Virtual Me: Origins turns things you really do into character stats. That only works if we can check evidence you send us — so this policy explains exactly what we collect, who processes it, and how to get rid of it.

Who we are

Virtual Me: Origins is made and operated by Virtual Me LLC, a Florida limited liability company (United States), 857 E Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy, Saint Cloud, FL 34769. For any privacy question or request, contact privacy@virtualmeorigins.com. For help with the app, write to support@virtualmeorigins.com.

Who can use the app

Virtual Me: Origins is for adults aged 18 and over. The app asks for your year of birth and will not let you continue if it indicates you are under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has used the app, email us and we will delete the account.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your address, phone number or payment card details.
  • We do not build face-recognition templates or any biometric identifier from your scans.
  • We never look through your photo library. We only see a photo or file you choose to send.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data. Ever.
  • Ads are never aimed at you, and advertisers receive nothing about you (see Ads on skill cards).

Who can see your details

Other players never see your name, email, age, sex, height or "About you" details. The person who runs Virtual Me (today, the developer) can see them in a private admin screen, together with your stats, proofs and avatar scans. They are used only to run the app: answering you, approving proofs that need a person, giving out avatar scans, and keeping the game fair.

Fair play

Every night, an AI looks over each account's recent activity — stats and how fast they climbed, actions and their titles, proofs and stat tests — to spot signs of cheating. It is given no names, emails or account IDs. When something looks wrong, it flags the account for a person to look at. If an account is put on hold, its stats stop climbing until the stat tests are taken again, and a note that this happened stays with the account on our side.

Ads on skill cards

Some skill cards have up to three spaces where a business that teaches that skill can show a plain ad: a name, one line and a link. Ads are not aimed at you — everyone who opens that card sees the same ones. We count how many times an ad is tapped, not who tapped it, and advertisers receive nothing about you. Advertisers are not part of Virtual Me, and you never have to use or pay one to prove a skill.

Connecting other services

Connecting is optional, and nothing is connected until you choose it. GitHub, Twitch, Google Calendar and X are connected by logging in and approving on that service's own screen. Duolingo has no login, so you put a short code in your Duolingo name once, which shows the account is yours.

  • What we read: only numbers about your own account. GitHub: which days you code. Twitch: which days you stream. Google Calendar: how many events you have each day and how long they last — never what they say. X: your follower count, how many posts you make and how many reactions they get, about once a week — we never keep or use what your posts say. Duolingo: your public streak and XP. We do not read your messages, your feed, your friends, or other people's accounts.
  • What we store: your username on that service, the access token it gives us (so we can read those numbers again later), and the numbers themselves. Tokens are kept on our server, never on other people's devices, and are used for nothing else.
  • What we never do: post, message, follow, like, or change anything on your behalf; sell or share this data; or use it to advertise to you.
  • Disconnecting: tap Disconnect in the app's Connected apps screen at any time. That deletes the token on our side at once. You can also remove Virtual Me's access from that service's own settings (for example Google's "Third-party apps" page, X's "Connected apps" page, GitHub's "Applications" page or Twitch's "Connections" page). For Duolingo, take the code out of your name.
  • Data from Google: our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Connecting a game

When you scan a game's QR code, you choose to share a read-only snapshot of your character — your stats, active perks, proven skills, connected app names, avatar files, and your height and avatar body measurements if you provided a height. Your name, email and age are not shared. Nothing is shared until you scan a code, the code expires after five minutes, and a game can never write back to your account or sign in as you.

How long we keep it

Your character data and avatars are kept while your account exists, because the whole point is a record that lasts. Proof files are kept so a reward can always be traced back to its evidence. Advertising requests are kept as a record of what was asked and agreed. If you ask us to delete your account, all of it goes.

Deleting your data

Email privacy@virtualmeorigins.com from the address linked to your account, or include your account ID from the app's Settings screen, and we will delete your account, avatars, proof files and stats within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to how it is processed. Email us and we will help. You can also withdraw a permission at any time in your phone's settings — for example, revoking camera or health access.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with access rules that restrict each account's data to that account. No system is perfect, and we will tell affected users promptly if a breach ever occurs.

Testing releases

This app is currently distributed to a small number of invited testers through Apple TestFlight and Google Play internal testing. Test builds may change often and may contain features that are incomplete.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who processes it — including before we connect a new service — we will update this page and change the date at the top.
